Live Australian time zones
Australia has three main standard time zones: AWST (UTC+8), ACST (UTC+9:30) and AEST (UTC+10). Daylight saving is observed in NSW, Victoria, Tasmania, South Australia and the ACT. Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory do not observe daylight saving. The map uses state/territory boundaries and labels the applicable time zone for each area.
